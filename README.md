# Modu

A browser-based "virtual phone" (小手机) for chatting with AI characters and companions.

Bring a character card or your companion persona and your chat history over, then start talking
and playing together in Modu. The character's memories are stored as plain files you can read,
edit and delete.

## 中文

Modu 是一个在浏览器里运行的“小手机”，用来和 AI 角色、AI 伴侣聊天。

把角色卡或你的伴侣人设，连同聊天记录一起带过来，然后在 Modu 里一起聊天、一起玩。角色的记忆是普通文件，你可以自己看、改、删。
