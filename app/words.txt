# Modu 小手机 — every line of text the app shows.
#
# How to edit: change only the text AFTER the "=" sign. Leave the name before "=" alone.
# Lines starting with # are notes and are not shown. Save the file, then reload the page.
#   {name}   becomes his name (for example Char)
#   {n}      becomes a number
#   {source} becomes where he came from (c.ai or 酒馆)
#   {folder} becomes the folder's name
#   {from} {to} become two dates
#   {why}    becomes the reasons, for example 2 条无法读取
#   {code}   becomes the lock screen's first code (DEFAULT in lock.js)
#   \n       starts a new line
# Not in this file: what he says (the model writes it), his memories (the model writes them,
# she can edit them in the app), and the dates and times (the phone's own format).
